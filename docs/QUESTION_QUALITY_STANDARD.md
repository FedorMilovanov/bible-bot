# Question Quality Standard

Mandatory before canonical promotion.

- Test one proposition per item.
- Keep all answer options in the same semantic class and at comparable specificity.
- Wrong options must be plausible, not jokes or unrelated categories.
- Do not let length, grammar, tone, qualifications, or scholarly vocabulary reveal the correct answer.
- A direct-text item must not hide an interpretation inside the correct option.
- Serious alternative interpretations must not be represented as absurd distractors.
- Greek distractors must be comparable lexical or morphological alternatives.
- Historical items must distinguish direct evidence from later comparison and reconstruction.
- Application items remain non-competitive.
- The explanation must stay within the sourced proposition and must not introduce a stronger unreviewed claim.
- Difficulty should come from knowledge and evidence discrimination, not from trick wording or answer shape.
- Every item must remain valid after option shuffling.

Automated schema/source checks are necessary but not sufficient. A chapter requires a deliberate wording and option-quality review before production promotion.
