# Ownership and editorial responsibility

**Repository owner and technical maintainer:** [FedorMilovanov](https://github.com/FedorMilovanov). The repository provides a Telegram bot and Mini App for studying the First Epistle of Peter; [README.md](README.md) is the technical and product entry point.

## What is owned and what is not

- **Product and engineering:** the course experience, implementation, CI contracts and maintenance of the repository are coordinated by the account owner, using human-directed software and AI-assisted tooling. Repository ownership does not imply that every source line, illustration or question was personally handwritten.
- **Editorial responsibility:** questions must be source-traceable, correctly classified as text, Greek, history, interpretation or application, and must distinguish the project's conservative theological position from uncontested facts. The owner is responsible for review and publication decisions, not for authorship of third-party ancient texts or scholarly sources.
- **Third-party materials:** biblical/Greek corpora, translations, commentaries, external libraries, platform APIs and provider services remain their respective owners' works and infrastructure. A citation or dependency does not confer redistribution rights.
- **User data:** Telegram user identity, attempts, statistics and other durable MongoDB state are operational data, not portfolio content. Do not surface them in public examples or screenshots without authorization.
